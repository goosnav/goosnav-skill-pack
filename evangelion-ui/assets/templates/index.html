Layout library

Six different information structures. Palettes, analog treatment and typography can vary independently. These are explanatory layout specimens, not finished apps.

  1. Operations console

    A command-led view with a narrow navigation rail, wide working region and subordinate detail pane.

  2. Analytical instrument

    A dominant plotting plane with a compact channel strip and a separate interpretation region.

  3. Decision network

    Chamfered decision panes around a central proposal; usable only when the product has actual participants or stages.

  4. Editorial dossier

    An oversized title, narrow contents margin and readable document body; no monitoring apparatus.

  5. Product catalog

    Image-led merchandise with a strong title band and a purchase/specification column.

  6. Process monitor

    One dominant current operation, a vertical sequence and a restrained event region.
